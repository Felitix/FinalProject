Nakivubo Wastewater Treatment Plant

The Nakivubo Wastewater Treatment Plant is a major sanitation infrastructure project in Bugolobi, Kampala.

  • Client: National Water and Sewerage Corporation
  • Location: Bugolobi, Kampala
  • Capacity: 45,000 m³ per day
  • ROKO's role: Consortium construction partner

The project was delivered through the OTV/Cementers/ROKO consortium.
